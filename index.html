About us

Cubic World of Taste is a delivery service for traditional Minecraft dishes. We deliver your desired meals anywhere in the Overworld at your scheduled time. From golden apples to suspicious stew — we bring the taste of the blocky world to your table!

The advantages of Cubic World of Taste:

  • We deliver food on time — no cobwebs delays!

  • We pack food in shulkers so it doesn't get damaged even in the Nether.

  • We deliver food 24/7 — even during thunderstorms.
